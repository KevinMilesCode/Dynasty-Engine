# Dynasty Engine Wiki

Community-maintained reference for the CFB27 Dynasty Engine app and mod.

**Source of truth:** this wiki is generated from the [`wiki/`](../../tree/main/wiki)
folder in this repo. Don't edit pages directly on github.com/.../wiki — edits
made there get overwritten by the next sync. Instead, open a pull request
against `wiki/*.md` here; once merged, it's mirrored here automatically.

## Pages

- [FAQ](FAQ) — common questions, curated by hand from answered
  [Discussions](../../discussions/categories/q-a)

## Getting help

- **Bug?** [Open an issue](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
- **Feature idea?** [Open an issue](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
- **Question?** [Ask in Discussions → Q&A](../../discussions/categories/q-a) —
  answered questions get folded into the [FAQ](FAQ) manually (see
  `MAINTAINING.md`), not by an automated bot.
